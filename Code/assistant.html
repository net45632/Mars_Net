How it works: The Q-Table is the "brain" of our Reinforcement Learning agent. It maps specific situations (states) to possible maneuvers (actions). The numbers you see are Q-values, which represent the expected future reward of taking a specific action in that state. As the simulation runs, the agent constantly updates these values—learning from collisions (penalties) and successful evasions (rewards) to discover the safest flight paths.
